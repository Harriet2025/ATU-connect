Everything campus communication needs, in one place

ATU Connect brings together the tools students and staff currently piece together across group chats, notice boards, and email.

Announcements Feed

Priority-tagged, categorized posts from lecturers, HODs, SRC, and admin — with likes, comments, and pinning.

Assignment Portal

Submit, track, and grade coursework with deadlines, resubmissions, and instant status updates.

Real-Time Messaging

Slack-style conversations with typing indicators, read receipts, and file sharing.

Smart Notifications

Never miss a deadline, cancelled lecture, or exam timetable release again.

Unified Calendar

Exams, department meetings, SRC events, and holidays in a single academic calendar.

Digital Notice Board

The physical notice board, reimagined — searchable, color-coded, and always up to date.
